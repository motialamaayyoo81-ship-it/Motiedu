Yaadannoo Guutuu fi Bal'aa Unit 1 & Unit 2

Unit 1: 1. DIKW Hierarchy & Data Formats

  • Data: Unprocessed, raw facts, figures, symbols, or signals without context. Example: "25", "Abdi", "Ethiopia".
  • Information: Data processed, organized, or structured into context to give meaning. Formula: $Information = Data + Context$. Example: "Abdi is 25 years old living in Ethiopia".
  • Knowledge: Application and internalization of information through experience, learning, and skill. Formula: $Knowledge = Information + Experience + Reasoning$.
  • Wisdom: Evaluating knowledge ethically to make decisions on when, where, and how to act.
  • Data Formats: Text, Numbers, Audio, Video, Graphics/Images.
Ibsa Bal'aa: Hierarchiin DIKW adeemsa ragaa bu'uuraa (data) irraa kaasee gara odeeffannoo, beekumsaa fi ogummaa ogummaatiin hojii irra oolchuutti jiru tarreessa.

Unit 1: 2. Hardware Infrastructure of IS

  • System Unit & Central Processing Unit (CPU): ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit), CU (Control Unit), registers, and system clocks.
  • Input Devices: Keyboards, Optical Scanners, Barcode Readers, Microphones, Touchscreens.
  • Output Devices: LED/LCD Monitors, Printers (LaserJet, InkJet), Audio Speakers, Projectors.
  • Storage Subsystems: Primary Storage (RAM, Cache Memory), Secondary Storage (SSD, Hard Disk Drive, Optical Media, NVMe).
  • Networking Equipment: Enterprise Routers, Network Switches, Access Points, Network Interface Cards (NICs).
Ibsa Bal'aa: Meeshaaleen 'Hardware' meeshaalee qaamaan qabataman kan ragaa fuudhuu, calaqqisiisuu, tursiisuu fi sab-qunnamsiisuu raawwataniidha.

Unit 1: 3. Software Architecture & DBMS

  • System Software: Operating Systems (Windows, Linux, macOS, Android) that manage memory, devices, and process scheduling.
  • Application Software: User-focused tools like MS Office, web browsers, graphics tools, and specialized business applications.
  • Database Management Systems (DBMS): Relational databases (MySQL, Oracle, PostgreSQL, MS SQL Server) for managing structured data records.
  • Security Software: Enterprise Antivirus (McAfee, Kaspersky) and Firewall protection networks.
Ibsa Bal'aa: Software'n ajaja meeshaalee hardware kompiotaraa komachuu fi adeemsisaa hojii geggeessuu dandeessisuudha.

Unit 1: 4. E-Services (E-Gov, E-Banking, E-Learning, E-Commerce)

  • E-Government: Digital platforms streamlining public tax filing, business registration, e-visas, and identity systems (e.g., Ethiopian National ID).
  • E-Banking: Electronic financial solutions: Automated Teller Machines (ATMs), Mobile Money transfers, Point of Sale (POS) terminals, and Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT).
  • E-Learning: Flexible virtual education using Learning Management Systems (LMS), digital libraries, video lectures, and online assessments.
  • E-Commerce: Digital platforms enabling buying and selling of physical or digital goods online (B2B, B2C, C2C).
  • Telemedicine: Healthcare consultation over video/audio bridging distance gaps for rural clinics.
  • Teleconferencing: Remote audio/video meeting tools (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet).
Ibsa Bal'aa: Tajaajilli E-Services teeknoolojii saayinsii odeeffannoo fayyadamuun tajaajila kaffaltii, barnootaa, fayyaa fi mootummaa tajaajilamtootaaf saffisaan dhiyeessa.

Unit 1: 5. IT Entrepreneurship & Case Study (RIDE)

  • IT Entrepreneurship: Innovation leveraging software and connectivity to solve daily socio-economic challenges.
  • Case Study - RIDE Ethiopia: Founded by Samrawit Fikru. Started with SMS technology before shifting to mobile app platforms.
  • Socio-Economic Impact: Created job opportunities for thousands of drivers, improved passenger safety, provided transparent fare calculation via GPS tracking.
Ibsa Bal'aa: RIDE fakkeenya guddaa teeknoolojii IT fayyadamuun furmaata geejjibaa fi carraa hojii uumuu fi biyya keessatti teeknoolojii guddisuuti.

Unit 2: 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Overview

  • Definition: Branch of computer science engineering systems to mimic human intelligence, cognitive logic, learning, and reasoning.
  • History & Evolution: Term coined in Dartmouth Workshop (1950s). Accelerated due to compute capacity growth and big data availability.
  • Primary Focus Areas: Reasoning, Problem-solving, Generalization, Knowledge Representation, and Perception.
Ibsa Bal'aa: AI'n saayinsii kompiotara sammuu namaa fakkeessuun sammuu uumamaa argarsiisuudha.

Unit 2: 2. Machine Learning (ML) & Robotics

  • Machine Learning (ML): Algorithms training on sample data to extract patterns and predict outcomes without explicit coding (e.g., Spam Detectors, Fraud Detection).
  • Robotics: Mechanical design of automated robots for tasks replacing humans (Industrial Assembly, Autonomous Vacuums, Surgical Assistants, Hazardous Exploration).
  • Space Exploration: NASA Robonaut assisting astronauts on the International Space Station (ISS).
Ibsa Bal'aa: Machine Learning koodii utuu hin barreeffamiin dataa irraa akka baratu kan gargaaru yoo ta'u, Robotics immoo meeshaalee socho'an uumuudha.

Unit 2: 3. Natural Language Processing (NLP)

  • Definition: AI subfield facilitating computer comprehension, translation, generation, and manipulation of natural human language.
  • Key NLP Applications:
    • Machine Translation: Google Translate, multi-language speech tools.
    • Virtual Assistants: Amazon Alexa, Apple Siri, Google Assistant.
    • Sentiment Analysis: Extracting emotional tone (positive/negative/neutral) from social media text.
    • Text Summarization & Fake News Identification: Verification of source credibility and condensing long papers.
Ibsa Bal'aa: NLP'n kompiotaroonni afaan namaa dubbatamuu fi barreeffamu akka hubatan, hiikan fi deebii kennan dandeessisa.

Unit 2: 4. Expert Systems (ES) & Structure

  • Definition: Software emulating decision-making abilities of human domain specialists.
  • 3 Core Components:
    • User Interface: Accepts queries from users and returns expert recommendations.
    • Knowledge Base: Repository storing domain rules in "IF-THEN-ELSE" logic formats.
    • Inference Engine: The reasoning brain executing logical rules against input queries.
  • Classic Example: MYCIN system identifying infectious bacterial diseases and recommending antibiotic doses.
Ibsa Bal'aa: Expert System'n ogummaalee ogeessota namaa fakkeessuun furmaata kennuudha.

Unit 2: 5. Augmented Reality (AR) & Virtual Reality (VR)

  • Definition: AR overlays digital graphics onto physical views; VR creates fully immersive simulated 3D environments replacing physical reality.
  • Comparison: AR keeps real-world background; VR completely shuts off physical reality.
  • Applications: Surgical training, remote tele-surgery, interactive museum exhibits, and interactive STEM education.
Ibsa Bal'aa: AR'n odeeffannoo dijitaalaa addunyaa dhugaa irratti fe'a; VR'n immoo addunyaa simulated ta'e keessatti guutummaatti si dhiyeessa.

Unit 2: 6. Data Science & Big Data Ethics

  • Data Science: Interdisciplinary field combining Statistics, Mathematics, Domain expertise, and Computer Science.
  • Big Data Metrics (3 Vs): Volume (Petabytes scale), Variety (Text, Audio, Video, Logs), Velocity (Real-time stream processing).
  • Privacy Techniques: Data Anonymization & Data Generalization.
Ibsa Bal'aa: Data Science'n dataa baayyee (Big Data) irraa beekumsa fi odeeffannoo calaqqisiisuu yoo ta'u, iccitii namaa eeguun murteessaadha.
